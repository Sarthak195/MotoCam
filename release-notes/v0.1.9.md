## MotoCam v0.1.9

Release date: 2026-03-31

### Highlights

- Added a recording preflight checklist to verify permissions, location services, camera readiness, writable storage, and basic device health before recording starts.
- Added an interactive playback route map powered by OpenStreetMap, with start/end/current markers and a collapsible map panel.
- Added fullscreen playback mode for distraction-free video review.
- Added timeline event markers for incident spikes and locked segments, including tap-to-jump behavior.
- Improved camera lifecycle handling around history navigation by releasing idle camera resources before opening history and reinitializing on return.
