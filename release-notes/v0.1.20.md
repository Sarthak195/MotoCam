### Upgrade notes / breaking changes

- TODO: state how the APK installs over v0.1.19 (signing key) once the artifact is built. Its versionCode (20) is above v0.1.19's (19). If Android refuses it over another MotoCam build, do not uninstall without saving your rides first: uninstalling deletes the rides in MotoCam's private storage.
- **Landscape recording is on by default.** While no ride runs, the UI follows the way the phone is held (the recording screen, Ride History, playback and Settings), and each ride is recorded in the orientation it was started in, kept from START to STOP. A build made with `--dart-define=MOTOCAM_LANDSCAPE=false` stays portrait-only, as before.
- The ride JSON gains one optional key, `appliedCaptureOrientation` (`portraitUp`, `landscapeLeft` or `landscapeRight`), written for every ride recorded with landscape recording on. A missing key means `portraitUp`; older readers ignore it, and no schema bump was needed. No existing file is rewritten.
- Settings is now a page of its own instead of a bottom sheet; the Share and Clear diagnostic log entries are in its ABOUT group.
- Installing over v0.1.13 or earlier: read the upgrade notes of v0.1.14 first (rides move to private storage and are no longer reachable over USB).

### Highlights

- **New look, "Instrument" (redesign A).** The whole app is drawn in one dark look with the bundled Barlow and Barlow Condensed fonts (OFL licence, listed under About and licences), a fixed set of colour tokens and large, glove-friendly controls.
  - Recording screen: a state band (READY, STARTING, REC, SAVING, LOCKED), status chips (a thermal chip from the platform thermal status among them), a details panel and START, STOP and LOCK under the thumb. STOP stays a single tap.
  - START opens a "Ready to record?" checklist page when something blocks or warns, with Fix buttons (app settings, location settings, battery exemption) and a recheck after each fix.
  - Ride History: Locked Clips first, month headers in All Rides, Locked, Incidents and Recovered filter chips, an integrity stamp per ride and an amber row for an incomplete ride.
  - Ride Playback and the export dialog follow the same look; every export note is kept under a collapsible "Before you hand this over" section.
  - Control labels that the phone test harness reads keep their accessibility text.
- **Landscape recording.** With landscape recording on (the default), the recording screen has a landscape layout: while idle a rail with Rides and Settings, the chips, the details panel and the live view in the middle, and START under the right thumb; while recording the REC band, the chips ending in Blackout, a live thumbnail with the speed, and STOP above a big LOCK under the right thumb. The preview, the pop-out (picture-in-picture) window and the ride file follow the ride's capture orientation, and crash detection gives the same decisions on portrait and landscape mounts. If the capture orientation cannot be locked, START is refused with a message, and the reason is written to the diagnostic log.
- **Private recording notification.** The recording notification is private on a secure lock screen, which shows a generic public version instead.
- **CI.** The test suite also runs with landscape recording off (`--dart-define=MOTOCAM_LANDSCAPE=false`), in CI and in the release precheck.
