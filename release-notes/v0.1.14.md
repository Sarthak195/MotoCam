This release covers everything on the `fix/scan-2026-10-20` branch since v0.1.13: the fixes from the 2026-10-20 whole-project scan (`docs/audits/2026-10-20-project-scan.md`) and the features that grew out of it.

### Upgrade notes: what riders must know

Read this before installing over v0.1.13 or earlier. The application id is unchanged (`com.example.motocam`); the earlier note about the signing key (v0.1.13.md) still applies to builds signed with a different key.

- **Your rides move automatically.** On Android, rides are now recorded to the app's private internal storage (`files/MotoCam Recordings`). After the update, MotoCam moves the rides from the earlier folders (the v0.1.13 folder under `Android/data/com.example.motocam/files`, and the documents and cache fallbacks) into the private folder in the background, whenever you are not recording. A banner in Ride History and on the idle recording screen shows the progress ("Moving N rides to private storage"). Files are moved byte for byte, so a moved ride keeps its LOCKED and DATA UNMODIFIED badges and its signature. Pressing START pauses the move; it continues after the ride stops. The move stops if the phone is short of space (it needs room for a whole ride) and waits for room on a later run. The move happens once for the rides present at the first run after the update. A ride you lock or unlock while it is still in an old folder stays there (listed and playable as before), because the move only takes files that are unchanged since its first run.
- **Rides are no longer reachable over USB or with a file manager.** Private storage is not visible to other apps, to a PC over USB or to file managers, on any Android version. To get a video out, use **Save to gallery** (below). Private storage is excluded from Android backup and device transfer, and it is deleted when the app is uninstalled or its storage is cleared (gallery copies stay).
- **The old public folder is not touched.** Rides recorded by v0.1.11 and v0.1.12 are in `Movies/MotoCam/Recordings`, where other apps can read them (including the GPS route files) and where they survive an uninstall. To bring them into MotoCam, open Ride History and choose **Import rides from an older version**: the app explains the folder and opens the system folder picker there. Tap "Use this folder" and allow access (the access is not kept). Only that folder is accepted. The files are copied into private storage byte for byte, so imported rides keep their badges and locks. Afterwards a dialog asks whether to delete the imported originals from the old folder; **Keep originals** is the default. Only the files of imported rides are deleted; a video without its ride file (for example a gallery copy you kept) is imported but never offered for deletion. The import never runs while recording, and START stops it (import again to continue). Gallery copies in that folder are separate files: removing them is a decision you make yourself, in a gallery app or file manager, or through **Remove gallery copies** for copies this version made.
- **Signatures are per install.** Rides recorded by this version are signed with a key that lives in the phone's Android Keystore. A ride shows as signed only on the phone and install that recorded it. A copy on another phone, or after uninstalling and reinstalling, shows only DATA UNMODIFIED, and after a reinstall older rides show as signed by another key. The signature shows the files were not edited by anyone without the app's key on the recording phone; it does not prove the footage is genuine, and a rooted phone or a modified app can sign anything.
- **Locked rides are never deleted automatically.** Not by the loop buffer, not by the new storage budget, not by the move or the import.
- **Clips now follow the bitrate setting.** On Android the bitrate setting used to be ignored: clips ran at about 20 Mbps at 1080p 30 fps whatever was chosen. They now use the chosen bitrate (default 8 Mbps), so they are about 2.5 times smaller and can show less fine detail, such as distant number plates. Pick 12 or 16 Mbps in the recording settings for more detail.
- **Background location is not requested.** The battery-optimisation exemption is offered once, after a saved ride, and never requested automatically.

### Summary

#### Recording

- **Screen-off recording.** While the recording notification shows, recording is designed to continue when the screen turns off, the power button is pressed or the app goes to the background without Picture-in-Picture. The camera stays open through the app's lifecycle handling and is released again as soon as capture stops. If the app comes back after 8 s or more in the background, it first checks whether the clip kept growing; if so, nothing is restarted and a note reads "Recording continued in the background." Otherwise it restarts the recording as before.
- **Shorter gap between segments.** A segment roll now keeps the camera's video stream running, so about 0.2 s of footage is lost at each roll instead of about 1 s (measured on a phone). This and the next item are patches to the camera plugin, which is now vendored in `third_party/camera_android_camerax` (`third_party/camera_android_camerax/MOTOCAM_PATCHES.md`).
- **The bitrate setting applies on Android** (the same fix as upstream camera_android_camerax 0.7.3). See the upgrade note.
- **Keep screen on while recording** (new setting, Android, on by default). Keeps the display awake during a recording, as the safe fallback if screen-off capture misbehaves on a phone. It applies from START and at once when changed during a recording.
- **Camera error reasons.** When the camera cannot start, the preview area names the reason instead of a spinner: camera permission needed, microphone denied (grant it or turn off audio), location permission needed, no camera found, the camera is busy in another app, or recordings storage unavailable, with Retry and Open Settings where they help.
- **LOCKED badge.** The badge on the recording pill now shows exactly while the segment being recorded is locked. The start checks (preflight) were reworked into one evaluator with the same rows.
- **Fewer false crash locks (jolt + confirm).** On a handlebar mount the old crash detection took vibration for crashes: one 13-minute test ride logged 52 incidents and locked every clip, and locked clips are never deleted, so a long ride could fill the phone. A jolt now has to last (vibration averages out) and only counts as an incident if, within 10 s, the bike nearly stops abruptly (from 20 km/h or more to under 8 km/h, much faster than a normal stop) or the phone stays tipped over for 2 s (bike on its side, phone thrown off the mount; leaning into turns and the side stand do not count). Without GPS only the tip-over counts. The lock, the vibration and the "Incident detected" notice now come at the confirmation, up to 10 s after the jolt; the notice still shows briefly over the Blackout screen. The lock still keeps the clips from before the jolt, and the ride's incident keeps the jolt's time and peak, so the playback marker sits at the jolt. Manual LOCK is unchanged. Replaying that ride's GPS speeds, none of the 52 jolts would have been confirmed.
- **Recording no longer fails to start on Android 14 and later with audio off**, and other scan fixes: roll failures surface as a recording fault, start and stop cannot overlap, and incident locks are applied at once.

#### Storage

- **Private storage and the automatic move** (see above). Recording needs no storage permission.
- **Import from older versions** (see above).
- **Storage budget** (new setting "Storage budget for all rides": Off, 4, 8, 16, 32 or 64 GB; off by default). While you record, when all rides in the recordings folder together exceed the budget, whole unlocked rides are deleted, oldest first, until they fit. Never a ride with a locked segment, the ride being recorded, a ride that a move or import left half-finished, or anything outside the recordings folder (old folders, gallery copies). If those alone exceed the budget, nothing is deleted. With the budget off, earlier rides are never deleted automatically. The free-space stop before START and at each segment roll remains the hard limit.

#### Gallery

- **Save to gallery** (Android). In Ride History, any ride's menu offers **Save to gallery**, locked or not. A dialog explains what a gallery copy means first (other apps can read it, it keeps the audio, it stays after an uninstall). Segments already in the gallery are not copied twice. The GPS route file is never copied.
- **Removable gallery copies.** MotoCam now remembers the copies it made, in a file next to the rides. When you unlock a ride, before you delete it, or from **Remove gallery copies** in its menu, it asks whether to remove them (**Keep copies** is the default). Android may ask for consent once for the whole ride. Copies made by earlier versions were not recorded and are not offered. On Android 9 and lower, a copy is removed only while it is still in `Movies/MotoCam/Recordings`; a copy moved to another folder is left alone.
- **Safer copying.** The copy queue is saved, so copies still waiting after the app was killed are made at the next launch, exactly once. On Android 9 and lower a copy is written under a hidden name and renamed only when complete, so a half-written copy never shows in the gallery. The opt-in **Copy locked clips to gallery** switch is unchanged (off by default).

#### Integrity

- **Signed rides.** The final save of a ride is signed with the phone's Keystore key (envelope version 3, ES256). Ride History shows who signed a ride, and a ride that this phone signed but that comes back unsigned or with another signature is flagged ("signature was removed or replaced"). Unlocking a ride signed on this phone signs it again; if that fails, the ride stays locked and the list says so. Rides from earlier versions, checkpoints and iOS rides keep the checksum badge only.

#### Ride History and playback

- **Faster Ride History.** Rides are read once and kept in a small index file, so the list opens without re-reading every ride file; the list no longer loads GPS samples it does not need.
- **Faster, cancellable video checks.** Segment checks run one at a time in the background, starting with the segment about to play, and their results are remembered, so an unchanged video is not checked again. Leaving the screen stops the check after the segment in flight. **Check videos again** forces a full re-check.
- **Playback.** The playback screen was split into smaller parts so that moving the position redraws only what depends on it. Behaviour is unchanged.
- **Map credit.** The map shows a tappable OpenStreetMap attribution, and the settings sheet has an About and licences entry.
- **Deleting a ride** (and the budget) now go through one routine that also updates the ride index, the signed-ride record and the gallery-copy record.

#### Build and project

- NDK pinned to 28.2.13676358, Kotlin Gradle plugin 2.2.20, backup and device transfer exclude recordings, CI builds the APK and iOS, checks formatting and runs tests with coverage.
- 591 tests in 43 files; `flutter analyze` reports no issues.
- `docs/` and the README were rewritten to describe the current code.

### Not in this release

- iOS has no native features (no foreground service, no Picture-in-Picture control, no signing, no gallery tools); this release changes no iOS native files.
- The fallback map tile source is unchanged.
- The application id stays `com.example.motocam`.

### Needs checking on a phone

This list was written before any device test. Some items have since been run on one Android 13 phone (2026-11-10 and 2026-11-11); the rest still need a phone. Please check on a real phone (at least one older Android 9 or lower and one Android 14 or later) before releasing:

1. **Screen-off recording.** Start a ride, turn the screen off with the power button and with the timeout, wait a few segments, turn it on: do the clips cover the whole time, with no frozen video, and does the notification stay? Repeat with Keep screen on turned off, and with the app on the home screen without PiP.
2. **The automatic move.** Install over a v0.1.13 build that holds rides (locked and unlocked, signed): does the banner show, do all rides appear after it, with their badges and locks, and does playback work? Try START during the move, a nearly full phone, and killing the app mid-move. Also lock or unlock a ride before it has moved: it should stay in the old folder and still be listed.
3. **Private storage.** Confirm the folder is not visible over USB or in a file manager, and that nothing asks for a storage permission to record.
4. **Import from older versions.** With rides in `Movies/MotoCam/Recordings` from v0.1.12, run the import on Android 9, 10, 11 and 14 or later: does the picker open in the right folder, is "Use this folder" accepted, is another folder refused, do the imported rides keep their badges, and does deleting the originals remove only the imported rides' files, leaving videos that have no ride file?
5. **Save to gallery and removal.** Save a ride, find the clips in the gallery app (with audio), save again (no duplicates), then remove them with and without the system consent prompt; test the delete-ride and unlock prompts and the Android 9 permission path. On Android 9, a copy moved to another album must not be deleted.
6. **Signed rides.** Record and save a ride: it should show as signed on this phone. Unlock it (re-signed), then edit or replace its JSON and confirm the badge. Check that behaviour on a phone whose Keystore key is missing or after a reinstall.
7. **Storage budget.** Set 4 GB with more than 4 GB of unlocked rides plus one locked ride: only whole unlocked rides go, oldest first, never the locked one or the current ride.
8. **Ride History speed.** With many rides (dozens), time the list opening after the first run; check that video checks start from the playing segment and that Check videos again re-checks all.
9. **Camera error reasons.** Deny the camera permission, deny the microphone with audio on, and open the camera in another app before starting: does each show the right panel and do Retry and Open Settings work?
10. **Resume check.** Leave the app for more than 8 s during a recording and return: is the "continued in the background" note shown only when the clip really kept growing?
11. **Notification Stop and kill.** Stop from the notification, and remove the app from recents during a ride: the last checkpoint should keep the ride, marked RECOVERED (INCOMPLETE).
12. **Battery exemption.** The offer appears once after a saved ride and is never requested automatically.
13. **A release build** (`flutter build apk --release` with the release key) installs, starts and signs rides; only debug builds were produced on this branch. Check also that it updates a v0.1.13 release install without an uninstall.
14. **Bitrate.** Record at 8 and at 16 Mbps: each clip's size times 8 divided by its duration should be close to the setting, and the ride JSON's `appliedBitrateMbps` should match it.
15. **Crash detection (jolt + confirm).** Ride the 2026-11-13 handlebar route at Medium: expect no or very few incidents, and the log lines `Telemetry: incident confirmed by ...` only for real events. Then, at a standstill with sensitivity High, hold the phone upright for a few seconds, swing it down fast onto a sofa cushion so that it lands flat on its back, and leave it there for 3 s: one incident, with the haptic pulse and notice about 2.5 s after the landing, also with Blackout on. The same swing caught upright in your hand, and laying the phone down gently, must not lock. In Ride History the incident marker should sit at the landing and the clip before it should be locked. If the swing never makes a jolt even on High, note it: the jolt filter may be too strict for falls.
