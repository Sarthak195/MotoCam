## MotoCam v0.1.12

Release date: 2026-04-05 (tag `v0.1.12`, commit `055b9e1`)

This release builds on v0.1.11. Storage, permission-recovery, timeline and segment-duration behaviour is unchanged from v0.1.11 and is described in `v0.1.11.md`.

*Superseded in v0.1.13: the v0.1.11/v0.1.12 public-folder storage (`Movies/MotoCam/Recordings` with `MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`) was replaced by app-specific storage (see v0.1.13.md).*

### Issue Summary

- Segment rollover caused a 200-900 ms UI hitch.
- In-app snackbar notifications cluttered the UI and blocked interaction with controls.
- The display and camera preview kept drawing power during long, stationary recordings.

### Resolution

- **Segment Rollover Performance Optimization:**
  - Reordered the critical path: stop recording, restart immediately, then register the finished segment and clean up.
  - Deferred retention cleanup to a background timer (2 s after the roll) to keep it off the critical path.
  - Deferred the Android media scan by 700 ms.
  - Per-component timing instrumentation: stop, restart, register and total ms are captured and stored.
  - The UI hitch is reduced; the encoder restart itself still takes about 1.0-1.3 s on the test devices, so a short gap between segments remains.

- **Notification System & UX Improvements:**
  - Replaced verbose snackbars with a minimal in-app popup (single line, auto-hides after 2 s).
  - The popup is a translucent box (35% black with a faint white border) inside an `IgnorePointer`, so taps pass through to the controls below.

- **Blackout Mode (Display Blackout):**
  - New recording-only display toggle in the header.
  - Pauses camera preview rendering via `CameraController.pausePreview()` to reduce GPU/CPU load.
  - Full-screen black overlay while recording continues.
  - Exit: hold anywhere on the screen for 2+ seconds. Blackout also exits automatically whenever the app loses focus (inactive, hidden or paused), for example when the notification shade is pulled down.

- **Playback and telemetry robustness:**
  - Ride playback skips missing or unreadable segments (re-probing the trailing segment once) and shows a one-time warning with the counts.
  - Ride telemetry keeps only segment paths that still exist and is written atomically through a temporary file.
